# PAYX Developer Portal

Integration documentation for the Pay CrossBorder (PAYX) cross-border payment APIs.

## About Pay CrossBorder
>Pay CrossBorder is a global payments infrastructure with registration number C100000654, enabling financial institutions, PSPs, and businesses to send, receive, and settle high-risk cross-border payments through Crypto and FIAT rails.

* Collections and payouts across 170+ countries
* Embedded AML/KYB
* Secure, transparent transaction flows
* Visit https://www.paycrossborder.com/ to know more.

## Overview

Pay CrossBorder powers cross-border payments for regulated Financial Institutions & Businesses that face more complexity and scrutiny due to the Higher Risk nature of their business.

Our platform is designed to simplify global payouts for sectors that traditional providers often avoid — but regulators approve.

The APIs described on this forum are limited to Financial Institution Customer.
